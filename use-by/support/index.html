Support

Use By Support

Email support@majesticmade.dev. Use By has no user account, so include your app version, device model, operating-system version, and a short description—not your full kitchen inventory.

Log and Prioritize

Add an item, choose its location and category, and edit the suggested date to match the package and your judgment. The Use First view shows the three nearest dates. Search or filter by location for the rest of the inventory.

Mark Used or Wasted

Complete an item as used or wasted to move it into local history and update the on-device use-rate summary. You can delete history from within the app.

CSV Export

Export creates a readable CSV through your device's share interface. It is not an encrypted vault backup. Review the destination and delete old copies you no longer need.

Local Password or Lost Device

Majestic Made cannot recover a forgotten local password or retrieve inventory from a lost device. Use By disables or excludes automatic operating-system backup and device-to-device transfer for its app-private data, and its readable CSV export is not a full-vault recovery file.

Purchase or Restore

Confirm you are signed into the Apple or Google account used for purchase, then use Restore Purchases in Use By. Subscription cancellation and refunds are handled by the applicable app store.

Food-Safety Notice

Suggested dates are organization defaults, not safety guarantees. Follow labels, storage directions, recalls, and official health guidance; inspect food yourself and discard it when in doubt.

Privacy Requests

Kitchen records are not stored in a Majestic Made account. Delete records in Use By or remove the app and its data through the operating system. See the Use By Privacy Policy for details.
