Use By Privacy Policy

Use By keeps kitchen inventory and used-or-wasted history in an encrypted vault on your device, without a Use By account or inventory cloud service.

Data on Your Device

Use By stores item names, quantities, categories, storage locations, optional barcode text, added and use-by dates, notes, and used-or-wasted outcomes in app-private storage. It calculates the use-first list and use-rate summary on the device. The vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM; its key and the local password verifier are held through platform secure storage.

App Lock

You may use a local password and supported Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint unlock. Biometric matching is performed by the operating system; Majestic Made does not receive biometric data.

Network and Purchases

Use By has no account, inventory server, cloud database, advertising, analytics, or remote crash-reporting service. RevenueCat and Apple or Google process subscription status and may receive store, product, device, and transaction identifiers needed for billing and restoration. They do not receive kitchen inventory from Use By. The current release does not scan barcodes, query Open Food Facts, recognize produce, send notifications, or synchronize with another phone.

CSV Export

CSV files are generated on the device. A file leaves Use By only when you invoke export and choose a person, app, or storage destination through the operating-system share interface. That destination's privacy practices then apply. CSV is readable and is not an encrypted vault backup.

Backups, Deletion, and Recovery

Use By does not run a backup or sync service and Majestic Made cannot recover a lost vault or local password. Device-backup behavior depends on your platform settings and is not guaranteed by Use By. You can delete items and history in the app, remove exported CSV files from their destinations, or delete the app and its local data through the operating system. There is no server-side Use By account for us to delete.

Support Messages

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not email your full inventory, exported CSV, payment credentials, or local password.

Contact
