Daymerit Terms of Use

Product documents: working draft

These documents describe a development build. They are not a release announcement. Product policies and store disclosures will be finalized before launch.

These terms govern Daymerit's parent-owned household account and connected parent, child-device, and family-board experiences.

Parent Responsibility

An adult parent or guardian must create and control the account, authorize household members and devices, choose age-appropriate routines, protect the parent PIN, and obtain any consent required for a child to use the service or submit photo proof.

Acceptable Use

Do not use Daymerit to harm, shame, exploit, surveil unlawfully, or expose a child; upload unlawful or abusive content; grant unauthorized access; bypass plan or security controls; or interfere with the service. Photo proof should be limited to what is reasonably needed for the task.

Points, Rewards, and Allowance

Points, rewards, and the optional allowance ledger are organizational records only. Daymerit does not hold money, execute payments, provide banking, or guarantee that a household member will deliver a promised reward or allowance.

Availability and Liability

Daymerit is a productivity aid, not medical, behavioral, educational, financial, or childcare advice. The service is provided as available and may change. To the extent permitted by law, Majestic Made is not liable for indirect or consequential loss. Non-waivable rights remain unaffected.

Termination, Changes, and Contact

We may suspend access needed to protect children, users, or the service, or address violations. Material term changes will be reflected by the updated date. Questions can be sent to support@majesticmade.dev.

Subscriptions and Local Trial — Working Draft

The approved US base-price target for Daymerit is $2.99 monthly or $24.99 per year, billed yearly. Core paid app access will be subscription-only, with no new permanent core-app unlock. Optional extra features or content may be separate one-time purchases. Existing records, basic exports and encrypted backup/recovery remain accessible after paid access ends.

Monthly and annual native subscription source is implemented in the development app; store product activation, real-store transactions and device qualification remain pending. The 14-day local trial requires no payment details and never starts billing automatically. A subscription requires explicit store consent and renews until canceled through that store; cancellation preserves access through the verified paid period, subject to bounded offline verification. After expiration, existing records remain readable and exportable, with encrypted backup/recovery available. Billing must not prevent data deletion; the current development app still requires a record-deletion workflow before release. Authentic verified historical permanent ownership remains honored, with no new permanent core unlock offered. Localized prices, renewal, cancellation, restore and refund terms must be shown before purchase. No payment is accepted on these pages.
