RoutineCue Privacy Policy

RoutineCue is a household account service. Unlike Majestic Made's local-only products, it requires cloud authentication and storage so family devices can share routines and task state.

Parent and Household Data

Supabase processes parent account identifiers, household settings, memberships, routines, schedules, task activity, points and rewards, optional manual allowance-ledger entries, notification rules, device-session records, and security or audit metadata needed to provide the service.

Child Profiles and Photo Proof

Parents create private child profiles using a nickname, avatar, age band, and display preferences—not child email, phone, legal name, exact birth date, or location. If a parent enables photo proof, selected images and related metadata are stored privately in Supabase Storage and made available only through time-limited authorized access. Proof retention is Free: 7 days, Plus: 90 days, and Pro: 12 months.

Notifications and Purchases

Firebase, Apple, or Google may process push tokens and delivery metadata for reminders; notification content is minimized. RevenueCat and the applicable app store process purchase and entitlement information. RoutineCue does not include ads, location tracking, public child profiles, child chat, or real-money movement.

Use, Sharing, and Security

Data is used to authenticate authorized family devices, provide routines and approvals, enforce plan limits, deliver notifications, prevent abuse, support exports, and operate the service. It is shared only with service providers performing those functions, recipients a parent authorizes, or when legally required. Access controls and private storage reduce risk but cannot guarantee perfect security.

Export and Deletion

The household owner can request a sanitized account export. Parent-controlled deletion can remove a child profile, individual proof, or the account. Account deletion removes household records, child profiles, device sessions, routines, task history, rewards, allowance entries, tokens, entitlement mirrors, and proof objects; local app state is then cleared. Some minimal security or transaction records may remain where required by law or a provider's independent obligations.

Contact and Access Help

Privacy questions can be sent to support@majesticmade.dev. In-app deletion is preferred. If a parent cannot sign in, support will verify account ownership without requesting a child's email, phone, exact birthday, legal name, location, or raw parent PIN.
