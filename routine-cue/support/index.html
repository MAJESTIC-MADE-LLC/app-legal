Support

RoutineCue Support

A parent or guardian can email support@majesticmade.dev with the app version, device and operating system, account email, and a short description. Do not include child photos, raw PINs, invite codes, or private routine details.

Sign-In and Family Devices

Confirm the parent account can sign in and that the device invite is current. Revoke an unexpected child-device or family-board session from parent settings. Support will never ask for a password, authentication token, raw parent PIN, or a child's personal contact information.

Photo Proof and Notifications

Parents control whether photo proof is enabled and can delete proof from the authorized parent view. Check operating-system notification permission and the rule schedule if reminders do not arrive. Avoid sending proof images to support.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases while signed into the store account used to pay. Billing, cancellation, and refund requests are handled by Apple or Google; subscription access may take time to resynchronize after restoration.

Export and Account Deletion

The household owner can export household data and permanently delete the account from the parent settings flow after parent verification and explicit confirmation. If the owner cannot sign in, email support; account ownership must be verified before deletion.
