What We Own information · Privacy · Terms · Support

Product status

What We Own is a concept for home inventory records. An app-specific privacy policy will be finalized before launch, based on the product that is actually offered.

Website and waitlist information

If you visit the portfolio, join a website waitlist, or submit an app idea, the website privacy policy explains the handling of those website submissions. That policy is separate from any future What We Own app policy.

Future app privacy information

Before release, the product policy will describe the applicable data practices and available privacy choices. Proposed features on concept pages do not establish current app collection, sharing, storage, security, or retention practices.

Questions and website data requests

Contact support@majesticmade.dev with privacy questions or to request an update or deletion of a website submission. Include the product name and the email address used for that submission so the request can be located.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, identity documents, private family information, or other sensitive records. Start with a brief description of your request.
