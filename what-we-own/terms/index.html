What We Own Pre-release Terms Information

What We Own is not released. Its single planned app combines home inventory with Claim Crate moving/PCS inventories, QR box labels, condition and damage photos, and selected claim-packet exports. This page describes its current concept status; app-specific terms will be provided before launch.

Last updated: October 23, 2026.

What We Own information · Privacy · Terms · Support

Current listing

What We Own is presented as a concept for home inventory records. The catalog describes development ideas, not an available app offering or a commitment to deliver the proposed features.

Availability and pricing

No What We Own app purchase or subscription is offered through these pages. There is no guaranteed release date, final feature set, supported platform, or price. Plans may change during research and development.

Website participation

You can review the concept on the Majestic Made website and use its available waitlist or feedback options. Joining a waitlist does not purchase an app or reserve a guaranteed release, price, or feature.

Website submission handling is explained in the website privacy policy. Avoid including sensitive records in messages or feedback.

Before launch and contact

Terms relevant to any released app, including applicable account or purchase arrangements, will be presented before launch. This pre-release notice does not set subscription, renewal, cancellation, or refund terms for a future product.

For questions about What We Own, contact support@majesticmade.dev.

Approved Subscription Target — Working Draft

The approved US base-price target for What We Own is $2.99 monthly or $24.99 per year, billed yearly. Core paid app access will be subscription-only, with no new permanent core-app unlock. Optional extra features or content may be separate one-time purchases. Existing records, basic exports and encrypted backup/recovery remain accessible after paid access ends.

These are approved planning targets, not a purchase offer or evidence that subscription billing is implemented or configured. Current development builds may still contain permanent-unlock billing and require migration and qualification before launch. Any trial, localized price, renewal, cancellation, restore and refund terms must be displayed by the applicable app store before purchase. No payment is accepted on these pages.
