RideBinder Privacy Policy

RideBinder is local-first. Cloud account, sync, document storage, and hosted sharing are optional paid services and are disabled in local-only builds.

Data on Your Device

Bikes, VINs, service records, reminders, checklist history, photos, documents, receipts, settings, and reports are stored locally by default. Files you export or back up go only to the destination you choose. Device-lock authentication is performed by the operating system.

Optional Cloud Services

If you enable paid cloud features, Supabase processes account identifiers, synced records, selected documents and photos, usage totals, entitlement mirrors, and security metadata. Hosted binders contain only the snapshot fields and assets you select; anyone who satisfies the link controls may view them. Cloud data becomes read-only when paid access ends and is scheduled for deletion after the documented 90-day period.

Purchases and External Lookups

Apple or Google and RevenueCat process store purchase and entitlement information. When you request a VIN or recall lookup, vehicle identity data is sent to NHTSA directly or through the configured service. If the separately disclosed cloud document-extraction option is enabled and you choose it, the selected document may be sent through Supabase to OpenAI for one-time field extraction after local OCR is insufficient.

Security, Sharing, and Logs

Cloud records are protected by authenticated access rules and private storage controls, but no system can guarantee perfect security. Service providers may process short-lived network, error, and abuse-prevention logs. RideBinder does not use an advertising SDK. Do not include sensitive files in a hosted binder unless disclosure is intentional.

Your Choices and Deletion

You may remain local-only, disable optional cloud features, export a report or encrypted backup, delete individual data, revoke hosted links, or delete the app's local data. Cloud users can delete their account in the app; this removes account data and uploaded files while leaving copies already exported or received by others outside our control.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@majesticmade.dev. Do not email VINs, titles, insurance files, backup passphrases, authentication tokens, or other sensitive records unless support specifically requests a redacted example.
