Support

RideBinder Support

Email support@majesticmade.dev with the app version, device and operating system, whether cloud services are enabled, and a short description.

Protect Sensitive Records

Do not attach unredacted titles, registrations, insurance cards, VINs, receipts, keys, tokens, or backup passphrases. If a sample is necessary, remove personal and vehicle identifiers first.

Local Data and Backups

Local-only records cannot be restored by Majestic Made. Export a RideBinder backup before replacing or resetting a device, retain the passphrase separately, and test the file before relying on it.

Cloud, Links, and Purchases

Check connection and account status, then retry sync before contacting support. Revoke a hosted binder if it was shared accidentally. Restore purchases through the app while signed into the store account that paid; billing and refunds are handled by the store.

Export and Deletion

Use in-app export for your records. Delete local records or the app for device data. Signed-in owners can use the account-deletion flow for cloud data; if access is lost, contact support and complete ownership verification.
