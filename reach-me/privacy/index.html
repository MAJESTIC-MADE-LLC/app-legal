ReachMe Privacy Policy

ReachMe works without an account. Contact profiles and browser drafts stay local unless you deliberately publish selected fields as a public link.

Local Profiles and Drafts

The iPhone app stores profiles and settings locally, configures its app and widget containers to be excluded from automatic iOS backups, and stores recovery keys and its purchase-owner identifier in device-bound, non-synchronizing Keychain storage. Portable encrypted profile backups and recovery-key files are created only when you request them and go to a file destination you choose. The web generator stores its draft in browser storage. Camera, Contacts, Face ID, file, and widget access are used only for the feature you request.

Optional Public Links

When you publish, the contact fields you select, an unguessable slug, a one-way device-owner identifier, and a one-way recovery-key verifier are stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. Anyone with the URL can view the published fields. Links expire after 12 months unless renewed and become eligible for automatic deletion 30 days later. Revoking a link deletes it immediately.

Purchases and Service Data

Apple and RevenueCat process purchase and entitlement information for paid features. Hosting providers may process short-lived network and security logs, including IP addresses. Abuse reports contain the link, selected reason, time, and a one-way network identifier. ReachMe has no advertising trackers and does not sell personal information.

Your Choices

You can clear a browser draft, keep iPhone profiles local, choose each field before publishing, revoke a link with its recovery key, delete local profiles, and remove exported files. ReachMe cannot recover a lost recovery key. Contact support if a public link must be removed and you no longer have the key; ownership verification may be required.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@majesticmade.dev. Do not include private contact data or a recovery key unless support specifically requests a redacted example.
