Almanac Privacy Policy

Almanac keeps collection inventory and tasting notes in an encrypted vault on your device, without an Almanac account or collection cloud service.

Data on Your Device

Almanac stores wine, whiskey, coffee, and cigar inventory; quantities; locations; prices paid; owner-entered current values; structured tasting notes; ratings; pairing history; and app preferences in app-private storage. The vault is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Its key and the local password verifier use device-bound platform secure storage.

App Lock

You may use a local password and supported Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint unlock. Biometric matching is performed by the operating system; Majestic Made does not receive biometric data.

Network and Purchases

Almanac has no account, collection server, cloud database, advertising, analytics, or remote crash-reporting service. RevenueCat and Apple or Google process subscription status and may receive store, product, device, and transaction identifiers needed for billing and restoration. They do not receive collection or tasting content from Almanac. The current release does not perform public-price lookups.

Encrypted Note Sharing

When you choose Share Note, Almanac places one selected note and its item context, including the sender display name you entered, in a passphrase-encrypted file. You select a destination through the operating-system share interface. The destination or recipient then controls its copy. Deliver the passphrase through a separate channel; Almanac does not transmit or recover it and has no transfer relay or friend graph.

Backups, Deletion, and Recovery

Almanac does not run a full-vault backup or sync service. This release disables or excludes automatic operating-system backup and device-to-device transfer for its app-private data, so those mechanisms are not a recovery path. Majestic Made cannot recover a lost vault, local password, or note-export passphrase. You can delete records in the app, remove shared files from their destinations, or delete the app and its local data through the operating system. There is no server-side Almanac account for us to delete.

Support Messages

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not email your vault, collection list, purchase records, or passwords. Use a redacted example if support asks for one.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to support@majesticmade.dev.
