Support

Almanac Support

Email support@majesticmade.dev. Almanac has no user account, so include your app version, device model, operating-system version, and a short description—not your private collection.

Local Password or Lost Device

Majestic Made cannot recover a forgotten local password or retrieve a vault from a lost device. Almanac does not yet provide a full-vault export and disables or excludes automatic operating-system backup and device-to-device transfer for its app-private data.

Share or Import One Note

Create an encrypted note file with a strong passphrase, share the file to your chosen destination, and send the passphrase through a different channel. To import, select the file and enter the exact passphrase. Majestic Made cannot recover it. Sharing or importing an entire vault and source-app CSV importers are not part of the current release.

Values

Current values are entered manually. The current release does not contact public valuation services and does not provide appraisals or investment advice.

Purchase or Restore

Confirm you are signed into the Apple or Google account used for purchase, then use Restore Purchases in Almanac. Subscription cancellation and refunds are handled by the applicable app store.

Privacy Requests

Collection records are not stored in a Majestic Made account. Delete records in Almanac or remove the app and its data through the operating system. See the Almanac Privacy Policy for details.
