Namefold Support

Product documents: working draft

These documents describe a development build. They are not a release announcement. Product policies and store disclosures will be finalized before launch.

Email support@majesticmade.dev with the app or browser version, device and operating system, and a description of the issue. Namefold does not use an account email.

Local Profiles

Local profiles remain on your iPhone and are excluded from automatic iOS backups. Use Namefold's manual encrypted backup before changing devices, retain its password separately, and restore from the file you selected. Majestic Made never receives that file and cannot restore a profile or recover its password.

Hosted Links

Use the recovery key to renew or revoke a published link. Revocation deletes the hosted record immediately. If the key is lost, email support with the public URL; we may require evidence before removing it and cannot reveal the key.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple account used to subscribe. Billing, cancellation, and refunds are handled through Apple.

Privacy Requests

Delete local profiles in the app and clear browser drafts in the browser. For a hosted-link removal request or other privacy question, contact support without sending unrelated private contact details.
