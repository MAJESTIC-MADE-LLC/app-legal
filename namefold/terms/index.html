Subscriptions and Local Trial — Working Draft

The approved US base-price target for Namefold is $1.99 monthly or $14.99 per year, billed yearly. Core paid app access will be subscription-only, with no new permanent core-app unlock. Optional extra features or content may be separate one-time purchases. Existing records, basic exports and encrypted backup/recovery remain accessible after paid access ends.

Monthly and annual native subscription source is implemented in the development app; store product activation, real-store transactions and device qualification remain pending. The 14-day local trial requires no payment details and never starts billing automatically. A subscription requires explicit store consent and renews until canceled through that store; cancellation preserves access through the verified paid period, subject to bounded offline verification. After expiration, existing records remain readable and exportable, with deletion and encrypted backup/recovery available. Authentic verified historical permanent ownership remains honored, with no new permanent core unlock offered. Localized prices, renewal, cancellation, restore and refund terms must be shown before purchase. No payment is accepted on these pages.
