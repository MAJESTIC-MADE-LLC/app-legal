Majestic-Made-Hub Privacy Policy

The Majestic Made portfolio site collects only the information you deliberately submit through a waitlist or app-idea form.

Information We Collect

A waitlist submission includes your email address, the product you selected, and a submission time. An app suggestion includes the idea, description, submission time, and an optional email address. Hosting and database providers may process ordinary network and security information such as an IP address, browser details, and request timestamps.

Use and Service Providers

We use submissions to send requested product updates, evaluate ideas, answer requests, secure the service, and operate the website. Supabase processes form records and our hosting provider serves the site. We do not sell personal information, use it for third-party advertising, or give data brokers access to it.

Storage and Retention

Form records are stored in a protected Supabase database whose public browser credential can submit but cannot read stored records. We retain records only while reasonably needed for the submitted purpose, legal compliance, security, or dispute resolution. Provider backups and security logs may expire on provider schedules.

Apps, Cookies, and Tracking

Joining a waitlist does not upload content from a Majestic Made app. Each released app has its own privacy policy. The current portfolio-site code does not intentionally set cookies or load analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking scripts.

Your Choices and Contact

You may ask to access, correct, or delete a submission by emailing support@majesticmade.dev. We may need to verify that the email address or record belongs to you.
