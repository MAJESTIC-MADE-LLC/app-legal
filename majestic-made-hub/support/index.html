Support

Majestic-Made-Hub Support

For catalog, waitlist, app-suggestion, or privacy help, email support@majesticmade.dev.

Website Problems

Include the page address, browser and operating-system versions, what you expected, what occurred, and a screenshot only if it contains no private information. Never send passwords, tokens, payment credentials, or unrelated app records.

Waitlist and Suggestion Requests

If a form does not confirm submission, verify the email and field lengths and try once more. The site has no user account or login. A waitlist entry requests updates but does not guarantee a product date, price, or invitation.

Privacy Requests

To request access, correction, or deletion of a waitlist or app-suggestion record, contact support@majesticmade.dev from the submitted email address when possible. We may request limited information needed to locate and verify the record.

Product Support

Use the product-specific support page for a released app. Do not send local-only app data through the website form; joining a waitlist does not upload content from an application.
