Concept questions

Creelmark is a concept for a fishing journal. Email support@majesticmade.dev with “Creelmark” in the subject to ask about the concept or share feedback. Features, availability, and timing remain subject to change.

Website problems

For a problem on the Majestic Made website, include the page URL, browser and device type, what you tried, and what happened. A screenshot may help if it does not expose private information.

No installed app, app account, version number, or purchase receipt is needed to ask about this concept.

Waitlist and submission requests

To ask about a waitlist entry, update submission details, or request deletion, email support@majesticmade.dev. Include the product name and the email address used in the website submission.

Review the website privacy policy for information about website submissions.

Keep messages safe

Send only the information needed to explain your question. Do not email passwords, sign-in codes, identity documents, private family information, or sensitive inventory records. A general description is enough to begin a support conversation.
